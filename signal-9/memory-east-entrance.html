East entrance

I was at the park bench near the east entrance when I saw you. Not close enough to pretend it was accidental. Close enough that two seconds would have been enough.

You looked like you were carrying something you did not want to put down. I had the message open on my phone. My mouth had already formed the first word.

I watched you walk past. I told myself you were in a hurry. I told myself later. Later never came with your face still in the glass doors.
