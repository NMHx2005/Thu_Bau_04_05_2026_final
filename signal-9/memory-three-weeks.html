Memory fragment — three weeks before.

I said something careless in the corridor. Not cruel. Worse than cruel: forgettable. You flinched and I pretended I did not see it.

I told myself I would fix it later. Later became a week. Then two. Then the sentence I rehearsed started to sound like an apology for taking too long, and I could not bear that either.

They probably forgot anyway. That is what I wrote in the margin of my own memory so I could stop returning to the moment. I am still returning.
